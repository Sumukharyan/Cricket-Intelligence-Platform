# 1. Cricket Batting Fundamentals

Batting is the skill of scoring runs while protecting the wicket.

A technically correct shot depends on:

- Head Position
- Footwork
- Bat Swing
- Timing
- Balance
- Follow Through

These factors will be measured in our Shot Analytics System.

---

# 2. Cover Drive

## Description

The cover drive is played through the cover region on the off side.

It is considered one of the most technically correct cricket shots.

## Key Mechanics

- Front foot moves toward the pitch of the ball
- Head remains stable
- Elbow stays high
- Full follow through

## Analytics Parameters

- Head Stability
- Shoulder Rotation
- Bat Speed
- Follow Through Quality

---

# 3. Straight Drive

## Description

A straight drive is played directly back past the bowler.

It is often called the textbook cricket shot.

## Key Mechanics

- Perfect balance
- Full bat face
- Stable head position

## Analytics Parameters

- Swing Path
- Timing Score
- Stability Score

---

# 4. Pull Shot

## Description

The pull shot is played against short-pitched deliveries.

The batsman hits the ball toward the leg side.

## Key Mechanics

- Fast shoulder rotation
- Weight transfer
- Horizontal bat swing

## Analytics Parameters

- Bat Speed
- Angular Velocity
- Shoulder Rotation

---

# 5. Cut Shot

## Description

Played against short and wide deliveries.

The batsman uses the pace of the ball.

## Key Mechanics

- Quick hands
- Good balance
- Precise timing

## Analytics Parameters

- Reaction Time
- Timing Score
- Wrist Position

---

# 6. Sweep Shot

## Description

Played mainly against spin bowling.

The batsman sweeps the ball across the leg side.

## Key Mechanics

- Knee bend
- Balance
- Bat swing arc

## Analytics Parameters

- Hip Rotation
- Knee Flexion
- Swing Angle

---

# 7. Important Biomechanical Parameters

## Head Stability

Elite batsmen maintain a stable head during impact.

Importance:

- Better timing
- Better shot control

---

## Bat Speed

Speed of the bat before impact.

Importance:

- Power generation
- Boundary scoring ability

---

## Follow Through

Continuation of bat movement after impact.

Importance:

- Shot completion
- Energy transfer

---

## Balance

Maintaining body stability while executing a shot.

Importance:

- Shot consistency
- Reduced errors
